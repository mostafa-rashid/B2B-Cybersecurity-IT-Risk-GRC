## Overview

A simulated GRC portfolio project demonstrating an integrated **cybersecurity, IT asset disposition, cyber supply chain risk, and business continuity framework** for Apex Solutions.

The project models security and operational workflows across data sanitization, hardware supply chain verification, business impact analysis, recovery planning, and disruption response.
## Project Objectives

- Establish secure IT asset disposition workflows
- Define data sanitization and verification procedures
- Assess supplier and hardware supply chain risks
- Document parts intake and verification processes
- Identify critical business systems and recovery requirements
- Develop business continuity and disaster recovery playbooks
- Define roles, accountability, and evidence requirements
- Align processes with recognized cybersecurity and compliance frameworks

## Frameworks

- **NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 1** — Media Sanitization
- **NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1** — Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management
- **NIST SP 800-34 Rev. 1** — Contingency Planning
- **ISO/IEC 27001**
- **R2v3**
